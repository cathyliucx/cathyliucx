## Hi there 👋

### 🇬🇧 English

I'm an AI Researcher with an interdisciplinary background in **Cognitive Neuroscience (MS)** and **Computer Science (PhD)**.

My research focuses on **self-improving collaborative AI agents** for complex research and decision-making. I study how agents can learn from feedback, experience, memory, and human collaboration to improve their reasoning, research, and decision-making capabilities over time.

My current work focuses on **AI for Investment Research**, including factor discovery, strategy research, backtesting, portfolio construction, and investment decision support. I also explore these methods in **AI for Science** and other knowledge-intensive research domains.

Alongside this direction, I am interested in **Human-Aware AI**, particularly multimodal understanding, long-term user modeling, and adaptive human–AI interaction—a research thread closely connected to my background in cognitive neuroscience.

My research focuses on **AI for Science**, particularly intelligent agents for complex research workflows involving hypothesis generation, evidence gathering, experimentation, validation, and continual improvement.

I study **self-improving AI agents** and **human-in-the-loop learning**, with an emphasis on learning from feedback, long-term experience, and human–AI collaboration.

My current work primarily applies these ideas to **quantitative investment research**, including factor discovery, strategy research, backtesting, portfolio construction, and investment decision support. I also explore their applications in scientific discovery and other knowledge-intensive domains.

## 🇨🇳 中文

你好！我是一名 AI 研究员，拥有**认知神经科学（硕士）**与**计算机科学（博士）**的跨学科背景。

我的研究聚焦于 **AI for Science**，重点关注面向复杂研究流程的智能体系统，包括假设生成、证据获取、实验、验证与持续改进。

我主要研究**自进化智能体（Self-improving AI Agents）**与**人在环学习（Human-in-the-loop Learning）**，探索智能体如何通过反馈、长期经验与人机协作持续提升能力。

目前，我主要将这些方法应用于**量化投资研究（AI for Investment Research）**，包括因子挖掘、策略研究、回测验证、组合构建与投资决策支持，同时也关注其在科学发现及其他知识密集型领域中的应用。

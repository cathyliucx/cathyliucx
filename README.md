## Hi there 👋

### 🇬🇧 English

I'm an AI Researcher with an interdisciplinary background spanning Cognitive Neuroscience (MS) and Computer Science (PhD).

My research focuses on building **Self-Improving Collaborative AI Agents** that can generate hypotheses, evaluate evidence, learn continually, and collaborate effectively with humans.

These directions share a common vision. **AI for Science** provides a general framework for automating scientific discovery and complex research workflows. **AI for Investment** extends this framework to quantitative investment research, particularly factor discovery, strategy validation, and investment decision support. **Human-in-the-loop AI** serves as the methodological foundation across both directions by incorporating expert knowledge, human feedback, and adaptive collaboration into continual agent learning.

My interdisciplinary background in cognitive psychology, cognitive neuroscience, and computer science enables me to connect human cognition, intelligent algorithms, and real-world applications.
